# Israel: The Birth of a Nation: v5 (visuals: day and night, better trees)

The user's request on 2026-10-16: "lets add day and night and i'd like better tree models they look bad". Their choices:
- **a real-time day/night cycle:** purely visual, while turns stay quarters;
- **all graphics modes:** SD gets a simpler, cheaper form.

This lifts the HD freeze for these two features only. Everything else in HD and HD+ stays frozen: bug fixes only. v4 shipped at `checkpoints/v4`; don't touch it.

When every box is checked, the lead cuts `checkpoints/v5` and the FULL game build (`build/full`, Windows + macOS), hands them over, and stops.

## Acceptance
- [x] **1. Day and night.** The sun (and the moon at night) moves in real time while the player plays: dawn, day, golden hour, dusk and night.
  - The default is about 8 minutes for a whole day. Settings → Graphics gets "Day length" (4/8/16 min), "Pause the clock" and a "Time of day" lock (dawn/noon/sunset/night or "cycle").
  - The cycle covers the sky colour and stars at night, the sun and ambient colour and angle, fog and haze, and the water (night reflections and moonlight).
  - **City lights at night:** windows glow, and there are street and port lights in the towns. It must stay readable: the map and labels are clear at night, and it is never pitch black.
  - The seasons (HD+) still apply on top.
  - It works in SD, HD and HD+. SD is the cheaper form: sun, ambient, sky tint and emissive windows, with no extra lights.
  - Captures and tests are deterministic: a fixed time by default under `--shot` / `--ui-test`, plus a `--time=dawn|noon|sunset|night|<hour>` flag.
  - The HUD is unaffected.
- [x] **2. Better trees.** New vegetation models for every species the map uses: Aleppo pine, olive, citrus, cypress, date palms (tall and cluster), Washingtonia, eucalyptus, oleander, bougainvillea, and any others in assets/models/vegetation.
  - **The look:** stylised and painterly to match the buildings and the Civ VI bar (`ref/screenshot.png`, `ref/hd_graphices.png`), with recognisable silhouettes. They are not blobs: real trunks and branching, leaf clusters with alpha cards where that suits.
  - **Seasons and wind:** HD+ foliage wind and translucency keep working, and the seasons tint them.
  - **Mode versions:** SD gets a cheaper version of each.
  - **Placement:** no black, soft or floating trees.
  - **LODs** and MultiMesh-friendly geometry.
- [x] **3. Performance and heat.**
  - HD+ ≥30 fps and HD ≥35 fps panning at 1080p, day and night. SD ≥50 fps.
  - Never worse than v4 by more than 10%.
  - Measure with the existing hd_cycle harness, keep runs short, and respect the GPU limits (the PC crashed at about 85°C).
- [x] **Tests:**
  - the v4 suites (Full control and story) pass with 0 failures and 0 SCRIPT ERROR;
  - the full sim_test is unaffected (visual-only change);
  - new checks for the day/night flags and settings.
- [x] **Blind sign-off:** a visual critic (Opus) judges before/after shots at the same cameras: Jaffa, Tel Aviv 2023, Jerusalem, the Galilee and the Negev, at dawn, noon, sunset and night, in SD, HD and HD+. Bar: "the trees look clearly better and day/night looks good and readable, with no artefacts", answered YES.

## Ownership
- **daynight** (Opus) owns:
  - the lighting and environment: main.gd's sun and environment, hd_render.gd, hdplus.gd (the season), the sky;
  - the night parameters in the water shaders;
  - the emissive window and light hooks in city_builder / building shaders;
  - the Graphics settings rows (a small edit in scripts/ui/settings_window.gd).
- **trees** (Opus) owns:
  - the vegetation: assets/models/vegetation, the vegetation parts of art_kit.gd and terrain_renderer.gd (`_veg_*`);
  - the foliage shaders (`props.gdshader`, the foliage part of hdplus.gdshaderinc).

Small surgical edits only in shared files; re-read them before editing.
