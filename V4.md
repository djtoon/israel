# Israel: The Birth of a Nation: v4 definition

The user's request on 2026-10-15: "the game is super complicated, we need the gameplay to change, same depth, but make it more fun to play, more casual, you are overwhelmed with options as you start and it's super hard to understand what to do."

v3 shipped at `checkpoints/v3`; don't touch it. v4 changes how the game is played, not what it simulates.

When every box below is checked, the lead cuts `checkpoints/v4` and the FULL game build (`build/full`, Windows + macOS), hands them over, and stops. There is no demo.

## The user's decisions (2026-10-15)
- **Story-first.** The player makes the big calls: forks, wars and fronts, peace and diplomatic deals, crises, coalition and budget PRIORITIES, programmes. Ministers run the routine automatically (city production, research and civics, policy slots, units, routine budget lines, trade routes, immigration placement and so on). The player can take over any domain at any time, and hand it back.
- **Keep quarters.** There are still 302 turns, but quiet quarters pass quickly: there is nothing to do but End Turn, or they auto-advance (an option).
- **Guidance:**
  - **Era goals:** 2–3 clear, sourced goals per era, shown on screen, with rewards.
  - **One clear next step:** one highlighted "what now" prompt instead of many notifications and buttons.
  - **A simpler screen:** a clean HUD at the start, with more appearing as systems unlock or are taken over.
- **Keep "Full control".** The new casual way is the default. A "Full control" setting (at New Game and in Settings) sets all ministers to manual, giving today's v3 game.

## Standing rules
- History is a corridor.
- The game ends after Q3 2023 with the memorial, verbatim, and nothing dated after Q3 2023.
- HD and HD+ are frozen: bug fixes only.
- GPU limits: max 2 Godot instances; start below 70°C; the watchdog kills at 81°C.

## v4 acceptance
### Play
- [x] **1. Story-first by default.** In the default mode, the player is never asked for routine choices: no "choose production", "choose research", "units need orders", "fill policy" or routine minister promotions. Ministers decide them, and each choice is visible in a short "your ministers did" summary.
  - Each domain has a minister toggle, auto or manual, which the player can flip at any time, mid-game included.
  - The automatic play is at least as good as Recommended for that domain. The default casual game played with history's story choices lands within ±5 points of v3's historical run.
- [x] **2. Same depth.** The six tracks, the endings, the collapses, diplomacy, forks, wars, programmes and the Treasury all still exist and still matter.
  - Opposite story strategies still end ≥10 points apart.
  - Divergence is ≥5 of 6 tracks.
  - The four strategists reach distinct endings, now playing only the story decisions with ministers on auto.
  - Full-control mode reproduces v3; the v3 sim_test still passes.
- [x] **3. One clear next step.**
  - Each turn the screen shows ONE primary prompt: the most important pending decision, or "End turn" when nothing is pending.
  - Other pending items wait in a small queue.
  - In casual mode a turn needs at most 2 player decisions on average and at most 4 at worst, outside war crises.
  - Core story density stays close to v3's: at least 1.0 per turn in every decade, with no decade below 70% of its v3 value.
- [x] **4. Era goals.**
  - Every era (1948 to 2023) has 2–3 goals, each grounded in real history with a source, such as winning the War of Independence, absorbing the mass immigration or building the National Water Carrier.
  - They show on screen with their progress, and give a clear reward when met: a track bonus, a programme discount or a story beat.
  - Missing a goal is a setback, never a game over.
- [x] **5. A simpler screen.**
  - At the start the HUD shows only the date, 3–4 key numbers, the next-step prompt, the era goals and End Turn.
  - The other panels (research, civics, policies, the Treasury, diplomacy, programmes, units and so on) appear when they first matter or when the player switches a domain to manual, each with one short introduction.
  - A first-time player in 1948 sees at most about 7 interactive elements.
- [x] **6. Quiet quarters pass quickly.** On turns with nothing to decide, End Turn is the only action. An optional "auto-advance quiet turns" setting is available. A whole game in casual mode takes far fewer clicks than v3; measure it and report the ratio.
- [x] **7. Onboarding.** The tutorial and How to Play are rewritten for the casual flow. The first 10 turns teach the loop: decide → see the consequence → end turn.
- [x] **8. Full control.** It can be picked at New Game and switched in Settings mid-game. It reproduces v3 behaviour, with the v3 UI tests passing in it.
